How Treos Connect handles your information

Treos Connect is an Android phone and dialer app built by Cordiso Technology. This page explains exactly what it accesses, why, and what it never does with your data.

What the app does

Treos Connect lets you make and receive calls, view your recent call history, and use standard call controls — mute, speaker, hold, answer, and end call.

Information it accesses

The app accesses phone and call-related information needed to provide this core functionality. If you grant permission, it can also access your contacts, so it can show contact names instead of raw numbers.

Phone state Call history Contacts (optional)

How that information is used

It's used only to power calling, the dialer, call history, and related in-call features. Nothing more.

Treos Connect does not record calls, require you to log in, sell your personal information, or use your call data for advertising or profiling.

Data sharing

This version of the app does not upload or share your call history or recordings with any third party for advertising purposes.

Permissions

The app may request phone, call history, and contacts permissions where they're required for its features to work. You're in control — manage any of these at any time from your Android settings.

Data security

Reasonable measures are taken to protect the information the app uses. Call history is managed by your device and stored by the Android operating system.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated if Treos Connect's functionality or data practices change. Check back here for the latest version.
